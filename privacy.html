Privacy policy

This website is an information page for Discorbit LLC. It collects very little, and this page says exactly what.

Effective 18 November 2026

Who we are

Discorbit LLC is a limited liability company registered in Wyoming, United States. Contact: info@discorbit.com.

What this website collects

  • No accounts, no orders and no payments are handled on this website.
  • The site sets no advertising or analytics cookies.
  • The enquiry form does not send anything to our servers. It opens your own email app with your message, which you then send to us yourself.

Email you send us

When you write to us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in the message. We use it to reply and to manage our dealings with you, and keep it for as long as that takes or the law requires.

Customers who buy from us on Amazon

Amazon collects and processes order and payment details under its own privacy notice. We receive only what is needed to fulfil and support an order, and use it for nothing else.

Services this website relies on

Pages are delivered by our hosting provider, which keeps standard server logs such as IP address and time of request. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the page loads.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us run the business, or when the law requires it.

Your choices

You can ask what information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by writing to info@discorbit.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version and its date will be published on this page.

Offer us your product line

Brands and distributors: send a price list and your reseller terms. We answer every enquiry by email.
