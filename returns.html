Policies

Shipping and returns

Every Discorbit order is placed on Amazon, so delivery, returns and refunds run through the customer's Amazon account.

Orders and payment

Discorbit LLC sells on the Amazon marketplace as a third-party seller. Orders are placed and paid for on Amazon. We do not take orders or card payments on this website.

Delivery

Delivery options, costs and dates are shown on the Amazon product page and at checkout. Tracking is available from Your Orders in the customer's Amazon account.

Returns

To return an item, open Your Orders on Amazon, choose the order and select the return option. Returns are accepted under Amazon's return policy, including its return window for the product.

Refunds

Once a return is received and processed, the refund goes back to the payment method used for the order. Amazon shows the status of each refund in the customer's account.

Damaged or wrong items

If an item arrives damaged, faulty or different from the listing, report it from the Amazon order page. We replace or refund it as Amazon's policy provides.

Questions

For help with an order, message us through the Amazon order page. For anything else, write to info@discorbit.com.

Offer us your product line

Brands and distributors: send a price list and your reseller terms. We answer every enquiry by email.
