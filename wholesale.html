Wholesale accounts for brands and distributors

We are looking for suppliers of home, kitchen and personal care products who sell to online resellers. Here is what we ask for and what we commit to.

Send an enquiry

What we ask a supplier for

Four documents are enough for us to place a first order.

  • Price list

    Wholesale case prices with product codes (UPC or EAN) for each item.

  • Order terms

    Minimum order, case sizes, lead time and how you ship.

  • Reseller policy

    Your minimum advertised price and any rules for selling on marketplaces.

  • Payment terms

    Accepted payment methods and when each invoice is due.

What we commit to as a reseller

We keep your price policy

Our offers stay at or above your minimum advertised price. If the policy changes, we update our prices when you tell us.

We leave your listings alone

We sell on your existing product pages and do not edit titles, images, descriptions or product details.

We sell only what you sold us

Our stock of your products comes from you or your authorised distributor, never from other sellers.

We stop when you say so

If you end the account, we stop ordering and sell through or return the remaining stock as you prefer.

Opening an account, in three steps

  1. Send your price list

    Use the contact page or write to info@discorbit.com with your catalogue and terms.

  2. Receive our company details

    We reply with our company documents and complete your reseller application.

  3. Get the first purchase order

    We choose the products we can sell well and send an order for them.

Offer us your product line

Brands and distributors: send a price list and your reseller terms. We answer every enquiry by email.
