How we work

From idea to launch.

Four stages, each with something you can see and approve before the next one begins.

  1. 01

    Discover

    We learn the business, the users and the constraints, then agree the scope, timeline and budget in writing.

  2. 02

    Design

    User flows, wireframes and a visual design you can click through before any production code is written.

  3. 03

    Build

    Short development cycles. Each one ends with a working build you can open, test and comment on.

  4. 04

    Launch and grow

    Release, app store submission or store go-live, followed by monitoring, fixes and new features.

Engagement 01

Fixed-scope project

A defined product or store with an agreed scope, timeline and price. You know the full cost before work starts.

Good for: a first version, a new store, a rebuild.

Engagement 02

Dedicated team

Designers and engineers working on your product month to month, with priorities you set and can change.

Good for: products that keep evolving.

Engagement 03

Launch and care

We keep an existing app or store healthy: updates, fixes, performance work and small improvements.

Good for: live products that need a steady hand.

Questions

Before you get in touch.

Something else on your mind? Write to info@discorbit.com

What kind of projects do you take on?

Web applications, mobile apps, games, desktop tools and online stores. We build first versions from scratch and we also take over products that already exist.

Do you work with clients in other countries?

Yes. We work remotely with clients worldwide. Communication is written and scheduled, so a difference in time zones does not slow a project down.

How do you price a project?

A well-defined project gets a fixed quote. Ongoing work is billed monthly. Either way you receive a written estimate before anything starts.

Who owns the code and the store?

You do. Once the work is paid for, the source code, accounts and design files are yours, and we hand them over in full.

Can you take over an existing app or store?

Yes. We start with a review of what is there, tell you plainly what we find, and propose a plan before changing anything.

How do we get started?

Send a short brief with the form below or by email. We reply with questions, then a proposal covering scope, timeline and cost.
