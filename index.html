What the company does

One line of business: we buy branded consumer goods in bulk and resell them online, one unit at a time.

Buys wholesale on invoice

We open reseller accounts with brand owners and their authorised distributors and pay for stock by purchase order.

Sells on the Amazon marketplace

Our offers appear on the brand's existing product pages. Customers order and pay through Amazon.

Keeps to each brand's terms

We sell only new goods in the maker's packaging and follow the pricing and listing policy each supplier sets.

Two product categories

We keep to everyday goods that sell all year and ship safely in the maker's own packaging.

Home and Kitchen

Goods for cooking, storing, cleaning and keeping a home in order.

  • Kitchen tools and food storage
  • Cleaning and laundry supplies
  • Home organisation

Health, Beauty and Personal Care

Sealed, shelf-stable care products from established brands.

  • Skin and hair care
  • Bath and body
  • Personal care and grooming

How an order moves, in four steps

The same route for every product we carry, from the supplier's invoice to the customer's door.

  1. Open a reseller account

    We apply to the brand or its distributor with our company documents and agree the terms of sale.

  2. Buy stock by purchase order

    We order by the case at wholesale prices and pay the supplier's invoice.

  3. Send stock for fulfilment

    Goods are checked, labelled and sent on so that orders can be picked, packed and delivered.

  4. Sell, then reorder

    Customers buy on Amazon. We track what sells and place the next order with the supplier.

What suppliers can expect from us

A reseller is judged by how it treats the brand. These are the rules we work by.

Read the wholesale account terms

  • Genuine goods only

    Every unit comes from the brand or an authorised distributor, with an invoice to show for it.

  • Your price policy, kept

    We sell at or above the minimum advertised price a supplier sets.

  • Your listings, untouched

    We do not change a brand's titles, images or product details.

  • Orders paid on your terms

    We pay invoices by the method and date agreed when the account opens.

Questions about Discorbit

For anything else, write to info@discorbit.com.

What does Discorbit sell?

Consumer goods in two categories: Home and Kitchen, and Health, Beauty and Personal Care. Everything is sold new, in the maker's original packaging.

Where can customers buy from you?

On the Amazon marketplace. Customers order, pay and track delivery through their Amazon account. We do not take orders or payments on this website.

Who do you buy from?

Brand owners and their authorised distributors, on a wholesale invoice. We do not buy stock from retail shops or unverified sellers.

How does a brand open an account with you?

Send us your price list, minimum order and reseller terms through the contact page. We reply by email with our company details and the products we would like to order.

How do returns and refunds work?

A customer starts a return from their Amazon order page, under Amazon's return policy. Refunds go back to the payment method used for the order.

Is Discorbit part of Amazon?

No. Discorbit LLC is an independent company that sells on the Amazon marketplace as a third-party seller.
