Company

An online retail company registered in Wyoming

Discorbit LLC buys home, kitchen and personal care goods wholesale and sells them to shoppers on the Amazon marketplace. That is the whole business, and we intend to do it well.

How the business earns its money

We buy products by the case from brand owners and authorised distributors at wholesale prices. We then offer those same products on Amazon, where customers buy them one at a time at the retail price.

The difference between the two prices, less Amazon's fees, shipping and storage, is our income. We hold no stake in any brand we sell and we do not make products of our own.

The company is at its start. We are opening our first wholesale accounts and choosing the first products to carry in our two categories.

Three rules for the stock we carry

Bought with an invoice

Every purchase is on paper from a named supplier, so the source of each unit can be shown.

Sold new and sealed

No used, opened, expired or repackaged goods. What the brand packed is what the customer receives.

Listed within the rules

We follow Amazon's seller policies and each supplier's reseller terms, and stop selling a product if asked by its brand.
